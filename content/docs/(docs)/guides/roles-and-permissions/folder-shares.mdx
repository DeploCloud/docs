A **folder share** applies inside one folder and everything under it. It **replaces** the
person's role there, and it **can give more** than the role does. For example, a **Viewer** can
deploy, configure and restart everything inside `Marketing`, and nothing anywhere else.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the folder menu

On the Overview, open the folder's menu and choose **Share folder**.

  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/folder-shares/01-folder-menu.png`}

/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick the member

Under **Add someone**, search by username and pick them. **People with access** above lists
who already has it.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/folder-shares/02-share-dialog.png`}

<Step>

### Choose the grant

Under **What can they do?** everything you may share is ticked. Untick what they should not
get, then **Share**. **View the team** is always included.

</Step>
</Steps>

To change a share, remove the person from **People with access**, then share again.

## What a share can give

- **Only what works on apps**: deploying, configuring, logs, variables, domains, backups, the
  console and the like, plus moving apps and organizing or deleting folders.
- **Never team-wide capabilities**: members, roles, tokens or databases.
- **Only what you hold yourself** inside that folder.
- **The most specific grant wins**: a grant on an **app** beats one on its **environment**,
  which beats one on its **folder**, which beats one on the **project**, which beats the role.

## Who can share a folder

The folder's owner, anyone holding **Manage team settings** (`manage_team`), and instance
admins. A person a folder is shared with cannot share it again. Creating a folder needs
`create_folders`.

## A folder is private

A folder is visible to its **owner**, the people it is **shared** with, members whose role is
limited to it, and team administrators. An app moved into a folder disappears from everyone
else's Overview until the folder is shared with them.

Removing a member revokes their shares. Folders they owned pass to the primary owner.
