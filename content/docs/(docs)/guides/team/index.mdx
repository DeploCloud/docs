---

## How it works

Everything in Deplo belongs to a team, and a person reaches it through a
**membership** that carries a **role**, a named set of
[capabilities](/reference/capabilities). Add or invite people in
[Members](/guides/team/members), see who did what in
[Activity](/guides/team/activity), and manage your own password,
two-factor and passkeys in
[Account security](/guides/team/account-security).

For access narrower or wider than a team role gives, see
[Roles and permissions](/guides/roles-and-permissions).
