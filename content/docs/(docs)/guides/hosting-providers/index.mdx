Any hosting provider can offer Deplo, partner or not. Both paths are good ones.

## How customers find you

New users start at [deplo.build/get-started](https://deplo.build/get-started). It asks three
questions: what they want to put online, how big it is, and who works on it. Then it lists
partner plans that fit those answers and the country they are in.

A click opens the partner's checkout in a new tab, next to that partner's steps to order the
server with Deplo.

## With a partnership

Partners are listed in that onboarding, with their logo and their plans.

- **An affiliate program with a recurring commission is required.** The plan card opens your
  affiliate link. No affiliate program, no listing.
- **Users are told.** The onboarding says Deplo earns a commission and their price stays the
  same, so list the price you charge everyone else.

## Without a partnership

Distribute Deplo preinstalled or as a one-click template, without asking us first. Deplo is open
source under the [GNU AGPL v3](https://github.com/DeploCloud/deplo/blob/main/LICENSE), so:

- Keep the license and the copyright notices.
- If you modify Deplo and let users reach it over a network, offer them the source of your
  modified version.

The full text of the license is what counts.

## Set it up

[Offer Deplo to your customers](/guides/hosting-providers/vps-template) is the setup we
recommend: a VPS template that installs Deplo on first boot, with the customer's account already
in it.
