## Open it

Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) anywhere in Deplo. Clicking the search box at the top of the
sidebar or pressing **/** does the same.

  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/search/01-palette.png`}

On a phone, the magnifier in the header opens it full screen.

## What it finds

Type a few letters and the palette searches everything at once. Case and separators are
ignored, so `better auth` finds `better-auth-docs`.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/search/02-results.png`}
  alt="The palette filtered by a query, apps, pages and settings together"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

| Group                           | What it covers                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Apps, Databases                 | Every app and database, by name, slug or id                      |
| Servers                         | Fleet machines, by name or address                               |
| Projects, Environments, Folders | The Overview's own structure                                     |
| Domains                         | A hostname, and the app serving it                               |
| Members                         | People on the team, by name or username                          |
| Roles                           | The team's roles, by name                                        |
| Cron jobs                       | Scheduled jobs, and what they run on                             |
| Templates                       | The one-click catalogue                                          |
| Pages                           | Every page in Deplo, New app included, and each app's own tabs   |
| Settings                        | Two-factor, passkeys, registries, cleanup and the rest           |

**Every row is a page.** **Enter** opens it and closes the palette. For an app or a database,
that is its Overview. The palette never deploys, starts or stops anything: those actions stay
on their own page, behind their own confirmation.

You only see what you are allowed to see.

## Reach one app's page directly

Type a page name to get it for every app. Add the app name to get only that one:

| You type              | You get                                            |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `deployments`         | Deplo's own page, then **every app's Deployments** |
| `deplo variables`     | **deplo-web / Environment**                        |
| `gamewatcher domains` | **gamewatcher / Domains**                          |
| `main backups`        | **main / Backups**                                 |

These rows show the app's logo with the page's icon on it.

A page also matches on its description, which is why `variables` finds the page called
**Environment**.

Six rows show at once. **Show N more** opens the rest.

<Callout type="info">
  A word that names one of your apps matches the app. Typing `blog` gives you the app, not
  all of its pages.
</Callout>

## Other teams

The palette searches every team you can reach. Results from the current team come first.
Others are listed under **Other teams**, each labelled with its team.

**Enter** on one of those switches team and opens the page in one step.

**Team settings are included.** Searching `roles` gives you the Roles page of every team you
can reach, and the roles themselves are searchable by name.

Teams you cannot enter right now, for example because two-factor is required, are skipped.

## Keys

| Key                 | What it does                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ctrl K** / **⌘K** | Open or close the palette                           |
| **/**               | Open the palette (unless you are typing in a field) |
| **↑ ↓**             | Move through the results                            |
| **Enter**           | Open the row                                        |
| **Escape**          | Close the palette                                   |
