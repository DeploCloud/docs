We are happy every time a hosting provider wants to offer Deplo, partner or not. Both paths
below are good ones.

## How customers find you

New users start at [deplo.build/get-started](https://deplo.build/get-started). It asks three
quick questions: what they want to put online, how big it is and who works on it.

Then it shows plans from our hosting partners that fit those answers and the country they are in.
One click takes the user to the partner's checkout, and the server arrives with Deplo already
on it.

## With a partnership

Partners get listed in that onboarding, with their logo and their plans.

- **An affiliate program with a recurring commission is required.** Your affiliate link is the
  one the plan card opens. No affiliate program, no listing.
- **Users are told.** The onboarding says that Deplo earns a commission when they buy through
  it, and that their price stays the same. List the same price you charge everyone else.

## Without a partnership

Any hosting provider can distribute Deplo, preinstalled or as a one-click template, without
asking us first.

Deplo is open source under the [GNU AGPL v3](https://github.com/DeploCloud/deplo/blob/main/LICENSE).
In short, you respect the license:

- Keep the license and the copyright notices.
- If you modify Deplo and let users reach it over a network, offer them the source code of
  your modified version.

The full text of the license is what counts.

## Set it up

[Offer Deplo to your customers](/guides/hosting-providers/vps-template) is the setup we
recommend: a VPS template that installs Deplo on first boot, with the customer's account
already in it.
