A role applies to the whole team. A **folder share** applies inside one folder: it **replaces**
the team role there, and it **can give more** than the role does.

For example, somebody with the **Viewer** role can deploy, configure and restart everything
inside `Marketing`, and nothing anywhere else.

The most specific grant wins: a grant on an **app** beats one on its **environment**, which
beats one on its **folder**, which beats one on the **project**, which beats the team role.
Members with such grants show a **Limited access** badge on the members list.

## Who can share a folder

Creating a folder needs the `create_folders` capability. Sharing one needs no capability: you
can share a folder you own, or any folder if you are a team or instance administrator. You can
only hand out what you have inside that folder yourself.

## A folder is private

A folder is visible to its **owner**, the people it is **shared** with, and team
administrators. An app moved into a folder disappears from everyone else's Overview until the
folder is shared with them.

Removing a member revokes their shares. Folders they owned pass to the primary owner.
