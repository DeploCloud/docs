A person joins a team as a **member** with a **role**, a named set of
[capabilities](/reference/capabilities). Deplo sends no email invites: you add somebody who
already has an account, or an instance admin creates a **registration link** for them.

## Add somebody who already has an account

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open members settings

Open **Settings -> Members**, then **Add member**.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/team-members/01-members-list.png`}
  alt="Settings -> Members: the member list with roles and permission counts"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Search, pick, assign

Search by username, pick them, choose the role, then **Add to team**.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/team-members/02-add-member.png`}
  alt="The Add a member dialog, picking a registered user by username"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}

/>

</Step>
</Steps>

Your team shows up in their team switcher immediately.

## Invite somebody new

This needs an instance admin.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start the registration

Click **Create a new user** in the **Add member** dialog, or **Register user** in
**Settings -> Users**. Then choose where they land:

- **They join your teams**: tick each team and pick **Member** or **Viewer**. From **Members**,
  the current team is already ticked.
- **They get their own team**: they name and own a new team when they open the link.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get the link

Click **Generate link**. **It works once and expires in 24 hours.** Send it over a channel you
trust: they pick a username, an email and a password.

</Step>
</Steps>

Unused links wait under **Pending registration links** in **Settings -> Users**, where you can
copy one again or revoke it.

## Roles and limited access

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/team-members/03-table-view.png`}
  alt="The same members as a table, role per person at a glance"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

Each row shows the member's role and how many permissions they hold. The badges:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary owner** | The team's founder. Nobody can remove, demote or edit them, not even an instance admin. |
| **Limited access** | Their role reaches only part of the team. See [Build a role](/guides/roles-and-permissions/roles#limit-where-it-works). |
| **More than role** / **Less than role** | Somebody gave this one person extra access, or took some away, on top of their role. |

Open a member to see three tabs:

- **Role & permissions**: the role, then where they can work and what they can do. Untick
  to take something away from this one person, tick to give them extra. The role and
  everyone else holding it stay as they are. Picking a role again resets them to it.
- **Activity**: what they did. See [Activity](/guides/team/activity#one-person).
- **Advanced**: transfer team ownership, or remove them.

Instance admin and the server grants belong to the account, not the team: an instance admin
changes them with **Manage account**, which opens **Settings -> Users**.

### Transfer ownership

Only the primary owner can, to another member of the team: from that member's **Advanced**
tab, or from **Settings -> General -> Danger zone**. Confirm with your password, your
two-factor code if you have one, and their username. You stay an owner, and only the new
primary owner can hand it back.

## Remove somebody

From the member's **Advanced** tab, click **Remove**. Only an owner can remove another owner,
and nobody can remove themselves or the primary owner.

- **They lose this team at once**: in the panel, over the API and through their tokens. Their
  account and their other teams are not touched.
- **Every folder share and per-project or per-app grant** they had here is revoked, so adding
  them back later starts clean.
- **Folders they owned pass to the primary owner**, so they do not vanish from the Overview.
  [Activity](/guides/team/activity) records the hand-over.

## Good to know

- **A team always keeps somebody who can manage it.** No role edit or removal can leave zero
  holders of `manage_members`, `manage_roles` or `manage_team`.
- **Teams are isolated.** Adding somebody here gives them nothing elsewhere.
- **Servers are shared between teams.** An instance admin picks which teams may deploy to each
  server.

## Troubleshooting

- **The user search finds nobody**: they have no account on this instance yet. Use a
  registration link.
- **A registration link says used or expired**: create a new one.
- **They are in the team but see nothing**: the team or their role
  [requires two-factor](/guides/team/account-security#when-your-team-requires-two-factor) and
  they have not set it up, or their role reaches almost nothing.

See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access) for more.
