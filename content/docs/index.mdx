Deplo is a ridiculously good alternative to traditional cloud providers.
Connect a repository, and Deplo builds, deploys, and scales it for you, no
servers to configure and no YAML to write.

<Callout type="idea" title="New here?">
  Start with the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart): your first
  deployment takes less than 5 minutes.
</Callout>

## Why Deplo

- **Zero config**: Deplo detects your framework and builds it correctly, every time.
- **Preview by default**: every branch and pull request gets its own URL.
- **Instant rollback**: any previous deployment can become production again in seconds.

  may change before the stable release.
</Callout>
