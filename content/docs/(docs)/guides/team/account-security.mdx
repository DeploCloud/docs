Everything is in **Settings -> Security**, which belongs to your account, not to a team. Two
tabs:

- **Sign-in**: password, two-factor authentication and passkeys. **Account protection** at the
  top shows what is set up and what to do next.
- **Devices**: where you are signed in right now.

**Sign in with your email or your username**, in the same field.

In the **Password** card, enter the current password and the new one twice, then **Change
password**.

- **Every other device is signed out.** With two-factor on, this one is too, and you sign in
  again with the new password.
- **The rules: 8 to 200 characters**, with at least one number, one lowercase letter, one
  uppercase letter and one special character.
- **It is checked against the Have I Been Pwned breach list.** Only the first 5 characters of
  the password's hash leave the instance. If the check cannot run, the password is accepted.

## Two-factor authentication

In the **Two-factor authentication** card, click **Turn on**. You need an authenticator app:
1Password, Bitwarden, Google Authenticator and Aegis all work.

1. **Confirm it's you** with your password.
2. **Scan the QR code** with the app, or type the **TOTP setup key** by hand, then **I have
   scanned it**.
3. **Enter the six-digit code** from the app, then **Turn on**. If one is rejected, wait for
   the next.
4. **Save the recovery codes**: **Copy** or **Download** them, tick **I have saved my recovery
   codes somewhere safe**, then **Done**. They are shown once, and each signs you in one time.

At sign-in you then enter the six-digit code, or a recovery code in `xxxxx-xxxxx` form.

- **New recovery codes** replaces the whole set. The old codes stop working at once.
- **Turn off** asks for your password and a code. It is disabled while a team or a role you hold
  requires two-factor, unless you also have a passkey.

## Passkeys

**Beta.** A passkey is a **full sign-in method**: **Sign in with a passkey** replaces the
password, and a session signed in with it counts as two-factor.

- **Add passkey** and **Remove** ask for your password. **Rename** does not.
- Passkeys need the panel's own address, over `https`, set in **Settings -> Deplo**.

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Synced** | Saved in a keychain, so it follows you between your devices. |
| **This device** | Only on the machine where you registered it. |
| **Security key** | A physical key you plug in or tap: USB, NFC or Bluetooth. |

A passkey is tied to the panel's address. If the panel moves, every passkey shows **Not usable
here**: sign in with your password, remove them and add new ones.

## Devices

The **Devices** tab shows your current browser at the top, then every other session under
**Other devices**, with browser, operating system, IP address and last activity.

## When your team requires two-factor

A team (in **Settings -> General**) or a role can require two-factor. Until you have it, that
team shows a lock screen instead of the dashboard, and you can do nothing in it: not from the
panel, the API, or any token you created.

From the lock screen you can turn two-factor on, sign back in with a passkey if you have one,
switch to a team that does not require it, or sign out.

## Good to know

- **External credentials are not breach-checked**: registry, SMTP, S3 and git tokens. A password
  Deplo generates is not checked either, because it is random.
- **Lost both the authenticator and the recovery codes?** An instance admin can reset your
  two-factor from **Settings -> Users**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The six-digit code is rejected**: the phone's clock is wrong. The code is time-based.

See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access) for more.
