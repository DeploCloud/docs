A role applies across the whole team. A **folder share** applies inside
one folder, and it **replaces** the team role's set there, and **may
exceed it**.

That combination is the point. Somebody on **Viewer** can be the person
who deploys, configures and restarts everything inside `Marketing`,
without gaining any of that anywhere else.

Most specific wins, over four rungs: a grant on a single **app** beats one on its
**environment**, which beats one on its **folder** chain, which beats one on the **project**,
which beats the team role. Members reached this way carry a **Limited access** badge on the
members list, because their role name no longer tells the whole story.

## Who can create and share one

Creating a folder needs the `create_folders` capability. **Sharing one needs no capability at
all**: you share a folder because you own it, or because you are a team or instance
administrator. What you may hand out is bounded by what you yourself hold inside that folder, so
a share can never manufacture access its author does not have.

## A folder is private

A folder is visible to its **owner**, the people it is **shared** with, and team administrators.
Move an app into a folder and it leaves everyone else's Overview until the folder is shared with
them.

A share ends with the membership: remove the member and it is revoked, and the folders they owned
pass to the primary owner.
