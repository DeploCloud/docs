**Activity**, in the sidebar, is the trail of who did what on your team,
and when.

---

## How it works

Every meaningful action is recorded, grouped by day, with the actor's
avatar: deployments, apps, projects, databases, domains, variables,
members, backups, destinations, cron jobs, cleanups, monitoring changes
and MCP access.

Actions taken by the system or by a git provider are attributed to
`system` or `github`, never to a person who happened to be nearby.

If an entry could not be written, the next successful write says so in the
trail, out loud, rather than leaving a silent gap. A hole in an audit log
has to be visible in the audit log.
