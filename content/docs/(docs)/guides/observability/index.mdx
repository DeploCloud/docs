---
title: Observability
description: Logs, metrics, health checks, alerts, security updates, a shell into a container, and scheduled jobs.
---

See what your apps are doing, and get told when something goes wrong. What runs on a server
reaches Deplo through its agent, over the connection Deplo already uses: no SSH, no exposed Docker
socket.

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Logs](/guides/observability/logs) | Live container output, and the output of every build |
| [Monitoring](/guides/observability/monitoring) | CPU, memory, disk and network, with a short history |
| [Health checks](/guides/observability/health-checks) | Whether an app is really working, not just running |
| [Notifications and alerts](/guides/observability/notifications-and-alerts) | Get told when something happens, in a chat, by email or on your phone |
| [Security updates](/guides/observability/security-updates) | Known flaws in what your apps run, and how to fix them |
| [Console](/guides/observability/console) | A terminal inside a running container |
| [Cron jobs](/guides/observability/cron-jobs) | A command on a schedule, inside a container |
