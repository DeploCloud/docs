**Settings -> Servers**, the server, **Cleanup**. One **Scheduled cleanup** policy covers every server,
including ones you add later; a server opts **out** with **Include this server**.

It ships **on**: every scope below, daily at `0 4 * * *` (UTC), a 24-hour minimum age and one image
kept per app. A scope added by a later release turns itself on in a policy you already saved.

- **Live apps are safe.** Every scope judges against what this Deplo still knows about, so a stopped app,
  or one moving between servers, keeps its images, files and network.
- **Swept after every deploy too:** **Unused app images** and **Build cache**, so a day of builds can't
  fill the disk before the schedule runs.
- **The build cache has a ceiling:** a tenth of the server's disk, between 2 GB and 50 GB. Past it, the
  oldest cache goes even when younger than the minimum age.
- **Never run:** `system prune`, container, volume or network prune. A container, and anything a
  container still uses, is never touched.

## What cleanup does not touch

- **Named volumes with data, and server folders an app reached through a
  [Bind](/guides/data/persistent-storage#what-deleting-the-app-removes).** Deleting an app does not free
  them: they are yours to remove.
- **Backup files.** Deleting a backup schedule leaves its copies in the
  [destination](/guides/data/backups-and-restore).
- **A migration source**, scheduled or on demand: that machine is somebody else's platform until you are
  done with it.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Cleanup frees nothing | The build cache is under its ceiling and the minimum age protects the rest, or what fills the disk is images your apps still use. |
| Rollbacks stopped working after a sweep | An app that can roll back keeps what its own **Keep** says: raise it there. **Images kept per app** only covers apps that can't. |
| A redeploy can't pull an image cleanup removed | The server has no access to that registry. Untick **Unused pulled images**, which applies to every server, or turn off **Include this server** there. |

More in [Servers and agents](/troubleshooting/servers-and-agents).
