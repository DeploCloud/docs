---
title: Buying Deplo from hosting providers
description: "Fix the common problems after buying a server with Deplo from a hosting partner: the email, the sign-in, the panel address and the bill."
---

import { Download, Globe, KeyRound, LifeBuoy } from "lucide-react";

You picked a plan on [deplo.build/get-started](https://deplo.build/get-started) and paid on the
hosting provider's checkout. The provider installs Deplo on your server and sends you its login.

| What | Who handles it |
| --- | --- |
| Payment, invoices, renewals, refunds, upgrades | The hosting provider |
| The server: power, reinstall, resources, firewall | The hosting provider |
| The email with your Deplo login | The hosting provider |
| Deplo, once you are signed in | [These docs](/), then the [issue tracker](https://github.com/DeploCloud/deplo/issues) |

## The email with your Deplo login has not arrived

1. **Wait up to 5 minutes.** The server boots and installs Deplo before the email goes out.
2. **Check spam and promotions** for `Deplo` and for the provider's name.
3. **Check the address on your order**, in the provider's client area.
4. **Check that Deplo is installed.** Some providers install it on their own; others need their
   install button, or the Deplo template. If you skipped it, do it now and wait 5 more minutes.
5. **Ask the provider's support.** They send this email, not Deplo, and can resend it.

<Callout type="info" title="Installing Deplo yourself? There is no email">
  The installer prints a setup link where you create your account: see
  [Create your first account](/getting-started/install#create-your-first-account).
</Callout>

## Your email and password are rejected

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| You used the Deplo login from the email | The client area login and the server's root password are other accounts |
| No space at either end of the password | A space copied by mistake is part of it |
| You already replaced the temporary password | Use the one you chose |

Some hosting panels can only pass the server's root password to Deplo: then the root password is
the temporary one. Still locked out? See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access).

## Deplo asks for a new password right away

Expected. At the first sign-in you replace the temporary password with your own and name your
team. Until then the account can do nothing else.

## The panel address does not open

The address is in the email, like `https://deplo-cb00710a.deplo.site`.

1. **Wait a few minutes.** It does not answer until the install finishes.
2. **Check that the server is running** in the provider's panel.
3. **Check the address.** `cb00710a` is the server's IPv4 in hexadecimal: `203.0.113.10` gives
   `cb00710a`.
4. **Open ports `80` and `443`** in the provider's firewall, if it has one.
5. **On your own domain**, its DNS must point at the server's IP. The `deplo.site` address works
   meanwhile.

Still nothing after 15 minutes? The provider can check the install from their side.

## The browser says the connection is not secure

Right after the install the certificate may still be on its way: wait a minute and reload. On your
own domain it needs the DNS pointing at the server and port `80` open: see
[Domains and TLS](/troubleshooting/domains-and-tls).

## The server has no Deplo on it

The provider's panel shows another operating system, or no Deplo panel ever answers: the order went
out without the Deplo template, or the install button was never pressed.

- **Install it from the provider's panel**, with their install button or the Deplo template.
- **Or install it yourself** with one command: see [Installation](/getting-started/install).

<Callout type="warn" title="A reinstall wipes the disk">
  Nothing to lose on a new server. If you already put data on it, take a snapshot or a backup first.
</Callout>

## The checkout did not open

After the countdown, deplo.build opens the provider's checkout in a new tab. If the browser blocks
it, the window says so: press **Open now**, or allow pop-ups for `deplo.build`.

## The price at checkout is different

- **Taxes:** VAT or other taxes can be added, depending on your country.
- **Currency:** deplo.build can show the price converted to your currency, as an estimate. The
  checkout charges in the provider's currency.

Buying through deplo.build never raises the price: Deplo earns a commission from the provider.
Invoices, renewals, refunds and upgrades stay between you and the provider: Deplo never sees your
payment.
