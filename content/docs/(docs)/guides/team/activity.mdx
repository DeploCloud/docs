**Activity**, in the sidebar (on a phone, under **More**), shows who did what on your team,
and when.

## The timeline

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/activity/01-timeline.png`}
  alt="The team timeline with its month groups and the 30-day breakdown"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

Newest first, grouped by month, with the month and its event count pinned at the top while
you scroll. More entries load as you scroll down.

- Each entry shows **who acted** (face and name), how long ago, and **what they did** in one
  sentence, with the exact time at the end.
- The app or database named in an entry shows its picture and links to it.
- The same action repeated by the same person is one entry, with one line per run.
- Actions by the system or a git provider are attributed to `System`.

- **Several values in one filter widen** the result, **two filters narrow** it.
- Every option shows how many events it covers.
- The filters are part of the URL, so a filtered view can be shared as a link.
- **Filtering by Resource hides team-level events**: adding a member or rotating a token did
  not happen *to* an app.

## The breakdown

**Events** and **People** list what the period holds, busiest first, five lines each. Click a
line to filter by it, click again to remove it.

- The heading names the period: the dates you picked, or the **last 30 days** when no date is
  set. The timeline itself goes back further.
- Each list ignores its own filter: picking a person narrows the events, but the People list
  still shows everyone's totals.

## One app, one database

Every app and database has its own **Activity** page, in its menu just above **Settings**: the
same timeline, already filtered to it. **Clear filters** brings you back to that page.

A database's own page shows only events recorded after that page was added. Older ones are
still on the team-wide **Activity**.

## One person

**Settings -> Members -> the member -> Activity** is the same timeline, filtered to them. It
needs **Manage members**, like the rest of a member's page.

## Events

| Group | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Apps** | Deployments, apps, security updates, projects, domains, variables |
| **Data** | Databases, backups, backup destinations, cron jobs |
| **Team** | Members and roles; tokens, passkeys and two-factor |
| **Platform** | Servers, integrations, instance settings, Docker cleanup, monitoring, MCP access |

## What you can see

- Activity needs the **View the activity log** capability (`view_activity`).
- A member whose role reaches only part of the team sees the events of what they can reach,
  not the team-wide ones.
- If entries could not be recorded, the log says how many, so a gap is always visible.
