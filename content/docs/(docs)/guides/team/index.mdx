title: Team
description: People, roles, the activity trail and your own account security.
---

A membership carries a role, and a role is a named set of capabilities. Everything a person can
do in a team comes from that set, plus any per-folder grant on top of it.

**Members** - add people, hand out roles, and take access away.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/members)

**Activity** - who did what and when, filterable, per team or per resource.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/activity)

**Account security** - your own password, two-factor, passkeys and signed-in devices.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/account-security)

**Roles and permissions** - the capability model, and sharing one folder.
[Learn more ->](/guides/roles-and-permissions)
