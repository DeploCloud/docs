Everything in Deplo belongs to a team. A person joins a team as a **member** with a **role**,
which is a named set of [capabilities](/reference/capabilities).

There is **no email invite**, because Deplo does not send mail. There are two ways in:

- The person **already has an account**: add them to your team directly.
- The person **has no account**: an instance admin creates a **registration link** and sends
  it to them.

## Add somebody who already has an account

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open members settings

Open **Settings -> Members**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a member

Click **Add member**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Search, pick, assign

Search by username, pick them, choose the role, then **Add to team**.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/team-members/02-add-member.png`}

/>

</Step>
</Steps>

They see your team in their team switcher immediately.

## Invite somebody new

This needs an instance admin.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open users settings

Open **Settings -> Users**, in the system section.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create the user

Start **Create a new user** and follow the wizard.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get the link

Deplo creates a single-use link. **It works once and expires in 24 hours.**

</Step>
<Step>

### Share it

**Share this link** with them over a channel you trust.

</Step>
</Steps>

They open it, choose a username and a password, and land in **their own team**. Then add them
to yours from **Settings -> Members**.

Pending links are listed on the same page and can be revoked before use. An admin can copy a
link again from the list during its 24 hours.

## Roles and limited access

Each member row shows their role. Two badges to know:

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/team-members/03-table-view.png`}
  alt="The same members as a table, role per person at a glance"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

- **Primary owner** is the team's founder. Nobody can remove, demote or edit them, not even
  instance admins. Ownership is transferred only from
  **Settings -> Members -> the member -> Advanced**, by the owner, to a member of that team,
  after entering the password and second factor again.
- **Limited access** means their real access is changed by
  [folder shares](/guides/roles-and-permissions), so the role name alone does not say
  everything.

Open a member to see **Permissions** (their role and the scope tree), **Advanced** (instance
admin, transfer ownership, remove), and **[Activity](/guides/team/activity)** (what they did).

A member whose permissions were edited by hand no longer follows their role. **Assign them a
role again** from the member page or the API to put them back on it.

## Remove somebody

From the member's **Advanced** tab. Their membership ends, their sessions in that team stop
working, and their API tokens lose the access they got from them. Their account and their own
team are not touched.

Two more things happen, so adding them back later starts clean:

- **Every folder share and per-app grant they had** in this team is revoked.
- **Folders they owned pass to the primary owner**, so they do not disappear from everyone's
  Overview. This is written to [Activity](/guides/team/activity), and the primary owner can
  share the folder again.

## Good to know

- **A team always keeps somebody who can manage it.** No role edit or removal can leave zero
  holders of `manage_members`, `manage_roles` or `manage_team`.
- **Teams are isolated.** Adding somebody to this team gives them nothing elsewhere.
- **Servers are shared between teams.** An instance admin decides on the server which teams
  may deploy to it.
- **Two-factor can be required for the whole team.** A member who has not set it up sees
  nothing in that team. See [Account security](/guides/team/account-security).

## Troubleshooting

- **The user search finds nobody**: they have no account on this instance yet. Use a
  registration link.
- **A registration link says used or expired**: create a new one.
- **They are in the team but see nothing**: the team requires two-factor and they have not set
  it up, or their role has almost no capabilities.

See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access) for more.
