---
title: Build a role
description: Name a set of capabilities, tick the ones it needs, and every member holding it updates at once.
---

import Image from "next/image";
import { basePath } from "@/lib/base-path";

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open roles settings

Open **Settings -> Roles**. Every role is on the left, with its permission count.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/roles/01-roles-list.png`}
  alt="Every role of the team with its permission count and how many members hold it"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Start a new role

Click **New role**, then **Start from scratch** (view access only) or a role under **Start
from an existing role**. Give it a **Name** and, optionally, a **Description**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Limit where it works

Under **Access**, everything is ticked. Untick what its holders should not reach, down to
single projects, environments, folders or apps. Leave it all ticked for the whole team.

A limited role loses the team-wide pages (Members, Roles, team Settings) and the capabilities
that only work team-wide: they stay ticked but count as **Out of reach** in the summary.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tick capabilities

Search, or browse by category. Each category has a **Select all** / **Unselect all** toggle for
what is shown. `view` is always ticked.

You can only grant capabilities you hold yourself: saving with one you lack is refused.

<Image
  src={`${basePath}/screenshots/roles/02-role-detail.png`}
  alt="One role open: name, two-factor requirement and the capabilities it grants"
  width={1728}
  height={1080}
  className="w-full rounded-lg"
/>

</Step>
<Step>

### Mind the sensitive ones

Capabilities marked **Sensitive** can destroy data or hand over access, for example deleting
things, opening consoles, revealing secrets, restoring backups, managing tokens or roles, and
connecting AI agents. The summary counts them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Require two-factor and save

Turn on **Require two-factor authentication** if needed: holders get no permissions until they
set it up, in the panel and over the API. Then **Create role**, or **Save changes**.

</Step>
</Steps>

- **Reset to default** restores Member or Viewer to what Deplo ships. Owner cannot be edited.
- **Delete role** removes one you created, once nobody holds it.
