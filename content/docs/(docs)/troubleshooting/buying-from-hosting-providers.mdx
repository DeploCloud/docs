You picked a plan on [deplo.build/get-started](https://deplo.build/get-started) and paid on the
hosting provider's checkout. The hosting provider installs Deplo on your server and sends you its
login. Most problems below fix themselves within a few minutes, or with one check in the hosting
provider's panel.

## Who does what

| What | Who handles it |
| --- | --- |
| Payment, invoices, renewals, refunds | The hosting provider |
| The server: power, reinstall, resources, firewall | The hosting provider |
| The email with your Deplo login | The hosting provider |
| Deplo, once you are signed in | [These docs](/), then the [issue tracker](https://github.com/DeploCloud/deplo/issues) |

## Email and sign-in

### The email with your Deplo login has not arrived

1. **Wait up to 5 minutes.** The server boots, Deplo installs, and only then does the email go
   out.
2. **Check spam and promotions.** Search your inbox for `Deplo` and for the hosting provider's
   name.
3. **Check the address on your order.** The email goes to the one in the hosting provider's
   client area, which may not be the one you use every day.
4. **Check that Deplo is installed.** Open your server in the hosting provider's panel. Some
   providers install Deplo on their own, others need you to press their button that installs Deplo,
   or to pick the Deplo template. If you never did, do it now and wait another 5 minutes.
5. **Contact the hosting provider's support.** They send this email, not Deplo: they can resend it
   and check the install from their side.

<Callout type="info" title="Installing Deplo yourself? There is no email">
  When the hosting provider's steps tell you to install Deplo with the guide, nobody sends a
  login. The installer prints a setup link and you create your account there: see
  [Create your first account](/getting-started/install#create-your-first-account).
</Callout>

### Your email and password are rejected

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| You used the Deplo login from the email | The client area login and the server's root password are different accounts |
| The password has no space at either end | A space copied by mistake is part of the password |
| You already changed the temporary password | Deplo replaced it at your first sign-in: use the one you chose |

Some hosting panels can only pass the server's root password to Deplo. In that case the root
password also works on the sign-in page, until your first sign-in.

Still locked out? See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access).

### Deplo asks for a new password right away

That is expected. At the first sign-in Deplo asks you to replace the temporary password with one
of your own and to name your team. Until you do, the account can do nothing else.

## The panel

### The panel address does not open

The address is in the email and looks like `https://deplo-cb00710a.deplo.site`.

1. **Wait a few minutes.** The install takes a few minutes after the server boots. Until then the
   address does not answer.
2. **Check that the server is on.** In the hosting provider's panel the server must be running.
   Start it if it is stopped.
3. **Check the address.** `cb00710a` is your server's IP in hexadecimal: each of the four numbers
   becomes two characters, so `203.0.113.10` gives `cb00710a`.
4. **Check the firewall.** Ports `80` and `443` must be open. If the hosting provider's panel has
   a firewall, allow both.
5. **On your own domain**, the domain's DNS must point at the server's IP. Meanwhile the
   `deplo.site` address works.

Still nothing after 15 minutes? The hosting provider can check the install from their side.

### The browser says the connection is not secure

Right after the install the certificate may still be on its way. Wait a minute and reload.

On your own domain the certificate needs the domain's DNS pointing at the server and port `80`
open. See [Domains and TLS](/troubleshooting/domains-and-tls).

## Deplo is not on the server

### The server has no Deplo on it

The hosting provider's panel shows another operating system, or no Deplo panel ever answers. The
order went out without the Deplo template, or the install button was never pressed.

- **Install it from the hosting provider's panel**, with their button that installs Deplo, or by
  reinstalling the server with the Deplo template.
- **Or install it yourself** with one command: see [Installation](/getting-started/install).

<Callout type="warn" title="A reinstall wipes the disk">
  On a new server there is nothing to lose. If you already put data on it, take a snapshot or a
  backup first.
</Callout>

## Checkout and billing

### The checkout did not open

After the countdown, deplo.build opens the hosting provider's checkout in a new tab. When the
browser blocks it, the window says so: press **Open now**, or allow pop-ups for `deplo.build`.

### The price at checkout is different

| Cause | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Taxes | VAT or other taxes can be added at checkout, depending on your country |
| Currency | deplo.build can show the price converted to your currency, as an estimate. The checkout charges in the hosting provider's currency |

Buying through deplo.build never raises it: Deplo earns a commission from the hosting provider,
and your price stays the same.

### Invoices, renewals, refunds and upgrades

All of it is between you and the hosting provider: Deplo never sees your payment. Look in their
client area or ask their support. A bigger plan is an upgrade with the hosting provider too.

## If you still need help

- **The server, the email, the bill:** the hosting provider's support. Tell them your order
  number and what you see.
- **Deplo itself:** the rest of [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting), then the
  [issue tracker](https://github.com/DeploCloud/deplo/issues).
