title: Team
description: People, roles, the activity log and your own account security.
---

A member has a role, and a role is a named set of capabilities. Everything a person can do in
a team comes from that role, plus any folder share on top of it.

**Members** - add people, give them roles, and remove access.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/members)

**Activity** - who did what and when, with filters, per team or per resource.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/activity)

**Account security** - your own password, two-factor, passkeys and signed-in devices.
[Learn more ->](/guides/team/account-security)

**Roles and permissions** - the capability model, and sharing one folder.
[Learn more ->](/guides/roles-and-permissions)
