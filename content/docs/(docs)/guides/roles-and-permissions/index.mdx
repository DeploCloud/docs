What each person can do comes from their **[role](/guides/roles-and-permissions/roles)**, or,
inside one folder, from a **[folder share](/guides/roles-and-permissions/folder-shares)**.

A capability is **one action**: `deploy_apps`, `delete_apps`, `manage_domains`,
`restore_backups`. There are 46: `view`, which every member has, and 45 you grant one by one.

Every team starts with three roles:

  type={{
    Owner: {
      type: "locked",
      description:
        "Full access. It cannot be edited, so a team can never lock itself out.",
    },
    Member: {
      type: "editable",
      description: "Deploy and manage apps, domains and variables.",
    },
    Viewer: {
      type: "editable",
      description: "Read-only access across the whole team.",
    },
  }}
/>

- **Editing a role updates everyone holding it** at once. Member and Viewer can be reset to
  their defaults later.
- **A role can be limited to part of the team**: some projects, environments, folders or apps.
- **A folder share replaces the role** inside one folder, and can give more than it.

## The floor and the ceiling

- **`view` is the floor.** Every member has it, and it cannot be taken away.
- **Your role is the ceiling.** A page it does not cover says so, for example **No access to
  logs**, and names the capability to ask for.
- **A limited role sees no team-wide page.** Members, Roles, team Settings, Storage and
  Monitoring read **Outside your access**.
- **A grant on one node replaces the ceiling there.** The most specific wins: app, then
  environment, folder, project, and the role last.
- **Some switches belong to the account, not the team.** An instance admin sets them in
  **Settings -> Users**: **Instance admin**, **Publish ports** (`canExposePorts`), **Bind server
  folders** (`canMountHostVolumes`) and **Configure Traefik** (`canConfigureTraefik`). See
  [Host access](/advanced/host-access-and-privileges).

## Good to know

- **Hidden buttons are not the security check.** The server checks every action, and the API
  and the MCP server go through the same checks.
- **A team can never lose its admins.** `manage_members`, `manage_roles` and `manage_team`
  must always have at least one holder.
- **An id from another team gives no result**, for security reasons.
- **API tokens are limited by their creator.** A token can never do more than the person who
  created it can do **right now**. Narrowing someone's role also narrows every token they
  created.

## Troubleshooting

- **Saving a role fails with "You can only give a role permissions you hold yourself"**: you
  ticked a capability you do not have. Untick it, or ask someone who holds it.
- **A ticked capability is greyed out**: the role is limited to part of the team, and that
  capability only works team-wide.
- **Somebody can do more, or less, than their role says**: check for a folder share on that
  folder, and the **More than role** / **Less than role** badge on their member page.
- **Somebody cannot do anything**: the team or their role requires two-factor.

See [Sign-in and access](/troubleshooting/sign-in-and-access) for more.
